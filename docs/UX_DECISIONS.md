# UX decisions

Every taste call made during the UX polish pass. Rules are untouched (see docs/DECISIONS.md).

## Foundation
- **Settings autoload** (`scripts/ui/settings.gd`, `Settings`): volumes (master/music/sfx/ui), mute, animation speed (`normal` / `fast` / `instant`), reduced motion. Saved to `user://settings.json`; defaults in `config/ux.json`. Tests and tools call `Settings.use_path()` with a temp file.
- **All UX tunables** (timings, volumes, speeds) live in `config/ux.json` or `scripts/ui/theme/`; no scattered literals.
- **Keep the "club room at night" direction** (walnut, ivory, brass, oxblood, ember, steel). Refine, don't restart.
- **Team sequencing** to avoid file conflicts: Phase A visual design + sound assets in parallel; Phase B game feel (match) + accessibility/meta screens in parallel; Phase C QA + independent critic; loop.

## Sound design
- **Palette**: wood knocks on felt for pieces, paper for cards, soft brass chimes for rewards/confirmations, a steel ring for shields, an FM "Rhodes" + upright bass for music. Everything is synthesized by `tools/audio/generate.py` (original, offline, deterministic); nothing downloaded.
- **Warm register**: no partial above 5 kHz, sample rate 22.05 kHz; every file's spectral centroid is under 2.5 kHz (`tools/audio/check.py`). Gentle attacks (4–10 ms) everywhere except impacts (wood, capture, explosion, shield).
- **Consistent loudness**: every one-shot is rendered to the same short-term loudness (-16 dBFS loudest 50 ms, peak ≤ -1 dBFS); the mix lives in `config/ux.json` → `audio.sounds[id].db`. Hover ticks sit ~16 dB under clicks; impacts and stingers are the loudest; music sits 6–9 dB under effects (match music quieter than menu music). A hard limiter on Master catches stacking.
- **Not annoying after 30 minutes**: non-tonal sounds get ±5–6 % pitch and ±1.5 dB variation per play; tonal cues (turn chimes, stingers, promotion, check) get none or ±1 % so they never clash with the music's key (C major / A minor). Per-id cooldowns (40 ms default, 60 ms hover, 250 ms turn/check cues, 1 s stingers) stop bursts from stacking; 8 SFX + 4 UI voices, oldest stolen.
- **Automatic button sounds** (hover tick + click) for every BaseButton, overridable with `set_meta("sfx", …)` (press only) and `set_meta("sfx_hover", …)` (hover only), so screens only add sounds for game events and outcomes.
- **Music**: `music_menu` is a slow ii–V–I lounge ballad (60 BPM, 32 s loop, sparse bell melody); `music_match` is a calmer A-minor modal vamp with walking bass and brushes (72 BPM, 40 s loop, no lead melody so it never competes with thinking). Seamless loops (tail folded onto the start), 1.5 s cross-fades between them.
- **Check** is tense but soft: a low minor-second swell over a dark gong, not an alarm. **Defeat** stingers descend gently; no buzzers anywhere (`ui_error` is a muted double knock).
- **Iteration 2: fatigue and laptops** (critic findings, sound column):
  - **Longer music in sections.** `music_menu` is now 128 s (A theme, A' high voicings + answering bell phrase + bass pick-ups, B bridge with a borrowed F minor 6/9, a 4-bar breakdown with no bass, a turnaround) and `music_match` 120 s (the vamp, a variation with rotating comping patterns, bass fills and ghost brushes, a lighter two-feel bridge, a 4-bar breakdown without kick). A 30-minute session hears each loop about 15 times instead of 45, and the breakdowns sit 2-4 dB lower so the loop breathes. I chose long rendered loops over Sfx shuffling segments at bar boundaries: one file per loop keeps the reverb tails and seam handling simple and exact. Music is mono 22.05 kHz now so the whole audio folder stays at 12.8 MB (stereo would have been about 22 MB); the old slow autopan was the only stereo feature.
  - **Variants** for the ten sounds that play many times a minute (piece lands, moves, pickups, captures, card draws/hovers, UI hover/click): 3 renders each with different seeds and ±5 % pitch/decay, picked at random and never repeated back to back, on top of the existing per-play pitch/volume jitter.
  - **Opponent turn chime toned down**: from a 1 s Rhodes dyad at -7 dB to a 0.4 s muted A3 tap at -14 dB with no ring-out. Your own turn chime stays clear but is gentler (softer attack, 0.9 s, -7 dB).
  - **Laptop presence**: `capture`, `explosion`, `check` and `piece_land_heavy` gained a short 1-3 kHz attack layer (wood snap, band-passed crack, a D6 brass ping for check). Measured as the share of the first 100 ms in 1-4 kHz: capture -19.6 → -11.2 dB, explosion -13.9 → -11.0, check -25.9 → -12.9, heavy land -21.8 → -13.1; centroids stay low (440, 219, 188, 286 Hz), so nothing got brighter overall. `check.py` now enforces ≥ -14.5 dB for these four. Not verified by ear on real laptop speakers yet.
  - **No clicks when voices run out**: the stolen voice fades over 15 ms on a spare player instead of a hard `stop()`.
  - **Stingers duck the music** 5 dB (Amplify effect on the Music bus, so the slider value is never touched) and release over 0.9 s afterwards.
- **Iteration 3: messages on small speakers, one stinger at a time** (critique 2):
  - **Refusals, cancels and "your turn" now carry a 1–1.6 kHz layer**: `ui_error` gets a muted, low-passed 1.1 kHz wood "tock" on each of its two knocks (presence −33.7 → −12.8 dB, centroid 203 → 260 Hz). `ui_cancel` gets a quiet brass blip a twelfth up (D6–A5, ~60 ms) that echoes its falling fourth (−26.5 → −13.4 dB, 346 → 372 Hz). `turn_player` gets short G5/D6 octave pings (−21.3 → −13.4 dB). Nothing got harsh: the layers decay in under 0.1 s and the centroids stay under 520 Hz. `check.py` now holds `ui_error`, `ui_cancel`, `turn_player`, `check`, `capture`, `explosion` and `piece_land_heavy` to ≥ −14.5 dB. Left out on purpose: `turn_opponent` (a deliberately background tap that the banner and the opponent's own move sounds back up), the stingers (long musical phrases with their melody at 330–880 Hz, always with a full screen) and the fire/transition swells (each has a visual). The reasons are in `tools/audio/README.md`. **Still not verified by ear on real laptop speakers**: the numbers say they should read, but someone has to listen.
  - **One stinger at a time** (`audio.stingers`): a new stinger fades out the one still sounding over 0.3 s. The music dip is re-timed to the new stinger, so a shorter replacement doesn't leave the music ducked for the old one's length. A lost match no longer stacks `defeat` under `run_defeat`. `unlock_fanfare` never cuts in: it queues behind a run stinger and starts when that ends. `Sfx.stinger_time_left()` lets the run-end screen schedule around it, and `Sfx.stop_stinger()` fades out and clears the queue.
  - **Hover ticks on every enabled button**: `sfx` controls only the press sound and `sfx_hover` only the hover. Buy, Accept, Decline, Skip, Start match, Go, Close and the other result-driven buttons (`sfx = "none"`) tick on hover like every other button.
- **Iteration 4: whose capture, and staying in check** (critique 3):
  - **`piece_land_medium_2` evened out.** At 380 Hz (the variant's ×0.95 pitch) the knock's second mode lands at 977 Hz, just under the 1 kHz band, so this one bishop/rook landing in three measured −19.2 dB presence against −11.7 / −10.8 for its siblings. It now carries the same kind of dry 1.24 kHz snap as `piece_land_heavy`. Its pitch, decay and body are unchanged: −11.4 dB now, centroid 430 → 458 Hz (siblings 451, 474).
  - **`capture_against`** (3 variants, −2 dB): the opponent took your piece. It is the capture family a fourth lower and darker: a 225 Hz knock instead of 300 Hz, a heavier felt thud, a low falling thump, a duller and shorter rattle at 360–450 Hz, and a muted 1.45 kHz snap instead of the 1.9 kHz one. The centroid is about 290 Hz against capture's 440 Hz, and presence is −11.0 to −11.7 dB, so it still reads on laptops. Your capture stays the bright one. The match plays it for the opponent's captures, guarded by `Sfx.has_sound`.
  - **`check_stay`** (0.48 s, −4 dB, no pitch jitter, 250 ms cooldown): a side stays in check instead of escaping. A felt clamp knock, then check's own D3/E♭3 minor-second dyad held flat for about 0.35 s and cut off, unresolved. A muted D6 tick gives it presence (−12.3 dB, centroid 238 Hz). It has no bells and no falling interval, so it can't be mistaken for `ui_cancel` (a falling G4–D4 bell fourth), which it replaces for this event.
  - Both are in `check.py`'s presence rule (≥ −14.5 dB) and rendered at the shared −16 dBFS short-term loudness. They have not been heard on real laptop speakers yet.

## Visual design
- **One spacing scale, base 4**: `Metrics` (`scripts/ui/theme/metrics.gd`) holds XS 4 · S 8 · M 12 · L 16 · XL 24 · XXL 32 · XXXL 48, screen margins (48 × 32), the match side-column width (272) and button heights (40 / 48 large). Screens use these instead of pixel literals.
- **Type scale** stays 12 caption · 14 body · 16 strong · 20 H2 · 28 H1 · 52 title, plus 76 `Display` (title screen only) and 28 `StatLarge` (Avenir/Helvetica lining figures for final scores). Georgia for headings, the default sans for body, lining figures for every number.
- **Contrast (WCAG AA)**: `IVORY_FAINT` raised from 8d8172 (4.3:1 on raised walnut, failing) to 9d907f (≥4.7:1 on every surface, including the lightest `WALNUT_HIGH`). `IVORY_MUTED` (2.1:1) is now only for disabled text and decorative glyphs; informational text that used it ("No artifacts", locked chips, map floor labels and captions) moved to `IVORY_FAINT`. New `OXBLOOD_INK` e0705c (≥4.5:1) for small oxblood text (refusals, hints, penalty tags, boss chip); plain `OXBLOOD` (3.5:1) stays for rings, fills and large headings (≥18.7 px bold), which only need 3:1.
- **Controls**: one button system (Button / PrimaryButton / QuietButton) with hover = lighter + brass edge, pressed = darker and the label sinks 1 px, disabled = flat sunk face + muted ink, focus = a solid 2 px brass ring drawn 3 px outside the control (`UiTheme.focus_ring()`), ready for keyboard focus in the accessibility phase. HSlider, CheckBox/CheckButton/OptionButton and PopupMenu are themed now so later settings screens match. `SelectTile` shows the same brass border when focused.
- **Locked and benched things sink instead of fading**: locked set tiles get a darker face and faint border with full-contrast text (only the piece row dims); roster rows on the board dim the token, not the text. Alpha fades were dropping the unlock conditions below 4.5:1.
- **Board marks are shapes first, colour second**: legal move = brass dot; capture = oxblood ring around the victim (plus a light wash) instead of a filled red square; movable piece = thin brass corner brackets; piece giving check = full oxblood frame + heavy corners; selected = brass wash + solid outline; last move = faint wash on the origin, stronger on the destination. A "Reading the board" legend in the match draws the exact same marks.
- **Pieces**: side is read by disc lightness (ivory vs ebony) first, the oxblood rim only confirms it. Check = doubled oxblood ring + a "CHECK" tag under the king (never colour alone). Shield = steel ring + a steel coin with a drawn heater shield (no font glyph). Burn = ember arc + ember number badge. Badge numbers use the lining-figure font.
- **Cards**: playable = raised walnut face, solid brass cost coin, ivory name; unplayable = sunk face, hollow cost coin, dimmed glyph and faint name (state no longer rests on 42 % opacity alone); hover = brass edge + 6 px lift; selected = 2 px brass edge, brass glow and 14 px lift. A thin inner rule makes the "notation slip". Upgrades are a brass ribbon, rare cards a brass lozenge under the name; Cheaper keeps the felt-green coin.
- **Map states**: next stop (glowing brass rim, dashed pulsing path from you), visited (brass fill, solid brass path), ahead (still reachable later: normal), out of reach (darker node, ghosted edges), and "YOU" tab + ivory ring on the current node. The next floor's label turns brass. The side panel shows the next stops when nothing is hovered (no dead space) and a two-column legend: node types and node states, with swatches matching the map's fills.
- **Match layout**: left = opponent (with a "Took" tray of captured pieces), match log, board legend; right = you (energy, penalty, "Took" tray, artifacts), then one "desk" panel that takes the spare height: the hint at the top (coloured bar + eyebrow title + instruction), the draw/discard/deck counts as figures at the bottom. The check-escape prompt replaces the hint inside the desk, so nothing below it moves and the column fits at 1280×720. Redraw / End turn stay at the bottom right, next to the hand.
- **Match over**: the final score is two big figures in each side's colour (ivory / oxblood) between rules, under the Victory/Defeat title.
- **Title screen**: a huge, barely-there chessboard under a lamp-like radial vignette (`TitleBackdrop`), the back-rank piece row with the opponent's ebony queen, a brass-flanked eyebrow, a 76 px Georgia "RogueChess" with a soft drop shadow, and a progress panel with small pip meters per unlock track.
- **Screen transitions** (`main.gd`): the new screen fades up and settles 14 px into place over 0.22 s (`Settings.time()` scaled; fade only with `Settings.reduced_motion()`). The old screen is freed immediately, so input is never delayed and no stale button stays pressable (tests rely on this).
- **Small fixes**: the reward screen no longer repeats the card name under the card (it shows what the card does + rarity instead); shop sections hug their content instead of stretching to the footer; artifact tiles say "CHOSEN" in words as well as with the brass border.
- **Aspect checks**: verified 1280×720 (= 1920×1080 under `canvas_items` + `expand`), 1280×800 (board and log grow, no gaps open) and 1706×720 (ultrawide canvas: side columns stay fixed, the board and the score race take the width).

## Accessibility & input
- **Settings panel** (`SettingsPanel`, `scripts/ui/widgets/settings_panel.gd`): opened by the HUD's **Settings** button, the title's **Settings** button, **F1** anywhere (toggles), and **Esc** wherever nothing else uses Esc (never on the match screen, which owns Esc for cancel; on the roster Esc first drops the selection). Four volume sliders with live "NN %" labels (5 % steps, a preview sound when a slider is let go), Mute, animation speed as three toggle buttons (the chosen one is a filled brass button, the others outlined: fill, not colour alone), Reduced motion, Close. Every change applies and saves at once (`Settings.set_value`); there is no Apply/Cancel step to forget. `ui_open` / `ui_close` sounds; the scrim, Esc, F1 or Close dismiss it.
- **Modal overlays trap focus** (`A11y.trap_focus`, using Godot 4.5+ `focus_behavior_recursive`): settings, deck, collection and the shop's removal chooser disable focus on everything drawn before them, swallow every key so nothing reaches the screen underneath, and give focus back to the control that had it when they close.
- **Keyboard on every menu screen**: `Ui.button()` stays unfocusable (the match wants Space for End turn), and menu screens call `A11y.focus_all(self)` after building. Each screen focuses its primary action on open (New Game, the first unlocked set, the first artifact, the map, the first reward card, the first affordable offer, Start match, Start new run), so Enter works straight away. The focus ring is the theme's 2 px brass ring drawn 3 px outside the control; Godot's default `show_focus_state_on_pointer_event` keeps it hidden after mouse clicks. `SelectTile` and the volume sliders draw the same ring themselves (outside the tile, so it never looks like the inner "selected" border).
- **HUD buttons stay out of the focus chain** (a clicked Deck button must not steal Space from the match): **D** opens the deck, **C** the collection, **F1** settings; their tooltips name the keys.
- **Number keys** pick directly where the choice is small and harmless: set select (1–9 pick), artifact select (1–9 toggle), map (1–3 travel; each next stop wears a small key cap with its number), reward (1–3 take). In the shop and coaching, where a key would spend points, 1–9 only **move focus** to that offer's button and Enter confirms.
- **Map keyboard**: the map takes focus; arrows walk the next stops left to right (selection and mouse hover are the same highlight, so they never disagree), Enter travels, and keyboard focus adds a brass ring outside the selected node. The map is custom drawn, so tooltips come from `MapView._get_tooltip()` (node type, opponent, reward, and whether it is a next stop, visited, ahead or out of reach).
- **Roster keyboard**: no board cursor (BoardView belongs to the match); instead Enter on a benched collection row places that piece on the first free deploy square (back rank first, centre files first) and Enter on a placed row benches it. Focus stays on the same piece's row across the list rebuild. Auto and Bench all remain one press away.
- **Tooltips wherever a rule matters**: map nodes and every legend entry, set and artifact tiles, shop offers (the card/piece rules plus the price, or "you have N" when unaffordable), card removal, coaching offers (item + upgrade rule) and their Accept/Decline, roster pieces, opponent pieces and the material line (scoring rule), HUD floor/points/deck/collection/settings, run-end "points left" (they do not carry over).
- **Minimum type size 12 px at 1280×720** on every screen I own: map floor labels and node captions went 11 → 12, the "YOU" tab 10 → 12 (tab widened to 36 × 20), map key caps are 12. Tooltips are 13. (Match widgets with smaller text are listed for the match agent below.)
- **Reduced motion** on the menu screens (`Feel`, `scripts/ui/feel.gd`): no shake, no scale pop, no brass glow, no travelling map marker (it fades in at the destination), no title drop (the piece row fades), the next-stop halo holds still instead of breathing; reveals become plain staggered fades. Refusals still explain themselves in the status line and play `ui_error`, so nothing is lost without the shake.
- **All menu timings go through `Motion.time()`** (named durations in `config/ux.json` → `motion`: `map_travel` 0.42 s, `chosen_pause` / `taken_pause` 0.22 / 0.3 s, `reveal` 0.3 s with 0.06–0.09 s stagger, `float` 0.9 s, `screen_transition` 0.22 s, `title_drop*`). Instant speed scales them to 15 %.

## Game feel
- **One timing vocabulary**: every match duration is a named entry in `config/ux.json` → `motion` (quick 0.12, move 0.28, impact_hold 0.05 …) read through `Motion.time()`, which applies `Settings.time()`; four eases (out = cubic out, settle = back out, in = quad in, soft = sine in-out). `Feel.pop/shake/fade_in/float_text/glow/reveal` read their durations from the same table and skip shake/pop/glow/travel under reduced motion. "Instant" (×0.15) still plays every step, so final states are always drawn.
- **Input first**: a click or key shows its result in the same frame (card lift, piece lift, the played card leaving the hand). Deals, banners, pip steps and float texts never block input; only the event sequence and the opponent's turn keep `is_busy()` true. End turn and Redraw disable the moment a sequence starts.
- **Weight by class**: pawn/knight land light, bishop/rook medium, queen/king heavy (bigger squash, matching `piece_land_*` sound, a 2 px board nudge for heavy pieces). Moves crouch for 0.06 s, hop in a short arc (knights higher), and land with a back-ease settle.
- **Hit-stop sparingly**: a 0.05 s hold only on direct captures, explosions and check. Explosion victims don't get their own hold. It is a *beat*, not a freeze: the event sequence waits (`MatchFx._hit_stop`) while the impact flash and ring that just opened keep growing, so the hit reads as landing rather than as a dropped frame. Freezing MatchFx tweens for under 60 ms would add bookkeeping for no visible gain.
- **Captures read as cause and effect**: the victim goes under the attacker, then flies away from the attacker's origin (or the blast centre) while shards scatter; the "+N" and the score track (filled to the event's running total, not the final score) follow.
- **Opponent turns stay legible**: each action shows the card it played face up beside the board, the moving piece's square (oxblood wash + outline) and its target (hollow oxblood ring), then the move. These marks are separate from the player's selection marks. The AI pause (`ai.move_delay_seconds`) is now scaled by the animation speed.
- **Turn handoff**: a brief banner across the header strip above the board (0.58 s total; see iteration 2) plus the turn chime; energy refills pip by pip with one `energy_refill`; spending drains pip by pip with an `energy_spend` tick each.
- **Hand**: only cards that weren't already shown deal in (from the draw-pile side, staggered 0.06 s), so Hold cards and the rest of the hand don't re-animate after every play; discards drop away before the new hand arrives.
- **Keyboard in the match**: 1–5 select a card, E / Space end the turn, R redraws, Esc / right-click cancel, Y / Enter escape when there is exactly one escape square (otherwise the hint asks for a click), N stays. Hints are key caps drawn on the cards and inside the End turn / Redraw buttons (button texts keep their prefixes), plus a caption line.
- **Drag to play**: dragging a card selects it; dropping it on a highlighted piece selects that piece, on a highlighted target plays it — exactly the click path, so both flows stay one code path.
- **Reduced motion**: no shake, nudge, hit-stop, squash, hop, particles, rings or token flashes; cards and pieces fade or slide straight; informational marks (square washes, check rings, CHECK tag, float text) stay.
- **Colour-blind cues on match widgets** (from the accessibility check): the capture ring has a light maple inner stroke so it reads on dark squares by contrast; the burn arc is dashed (one dashed third per turn left) so it never reads like a solid brass mark; Cheaper cards carry a down-arrow tab on the cost coin; card key caps, names, cost figures and the upgrade ribbon are at least 12 px (the ribbon moves under the coin only when it can't fit beside it; mini cards can drop the coin with `show_cost = false`), and so is the CHECK tag.
- **Iteration 2, match feel** (critique 1):
  - *Match over leads with the reason*: a king capture shows a toppled king and "Checkmate: you captured the king"; the big count-up score appears only when the score decided the match (target or turn limit) and isn't 0–0, otherwise a small "Final score" caption (hidden at 0–0). The "–" uses the figures' font so it sits on their centre.
  - *Turn banner in the header strip*, covering the turn label and score race above the board, never the pieces. The opponent's first action waits `max(move delay, banner time left)` (a MatchFx-owned timer, so it follows the animation speed and `Engine.time_scale`). Its revealed card sits left of the rank numbers (`BoardView.COORD_GUTTER`).
  - *Opponent card backs* use the card language: walnut face + rule, oxblood field, brass inner rule and lozenge (24 × 34; since opponent card visibility they are 40 × 56 minis, the same size as the shown face cards, see below).
  - *One beat per explosion*: the explosion plays its flash, ring, shake and a single hit-stop, then every blast victim (and shield block) resolves in the same frame; "Boom!" and the victims' "+N" appear together and the blast gets one `explosion_beat` (0.3 s). Rings and shards are drawn in a clip over the board frame.

## Code structure
- **One home per concern for feedback**: `Motion` (`scripts/ui/fx/motion.gd`) owns timings and eases (every duration is a name in `config/ux.json` → `motion`); `Feel` (`scripts/ui/feel.gd`) owns the effect helpers used by every screen (`pop`, `shake`, `refuse`, `flash_status`, `fade_in`, `float_text`, `glow`, `reveal`, `centre_in`); `Ui` is builders only.
- **Pop keeps its two tunings** rather than silently retiming one: `Feel.pop(node, amount, kind)` reads `<kind>_in` / `<kind>_out` — `menu_pop` (0.064 / 0.16 s) on the menus, `pop` (0.09 / 0.15 s) in the match.
- **Float text under reduced motion only fades** (no 12 px drift) on every screen, matching "nothing travels" for the menus; the text itself still appears.
- **Overlay fade** uses `overlay_fade` (0.15 s) once; the overlays used to scale it twice (`Settings.time(Settings.time(..))`), so it was shorter than intended on Fast / Instant.
- Run end: the lounge loop returns after `Sfx.stinger_time_left()` (the run stinger, 3.8 s, then the fanfare when there is one), never a hand-copied length; audio time, so the animation speed never scales it.
- **Match screen split** (no behaviour change): `match_screen.gd` keeps input, selection and the play / opponent loop (every handler and field the tests and screenshot tool use); `scripts/ui/match/match_view.gd` (`MatchView`) builds the widgets and refreshes them from the game; `scripts/ui/match/match_fx.gd` (`MatchFx`, a child node, so its tweens and awaits die with the screen) animates event lists; `scripts/ui/match/match_over_panel.gd` is the result modal.
- **Modal overlays**: `A11y.trap_focus` puts the overlay in the `modal_overlay` group until it is released; `A11y.is_modal_open()` / `main.overlay_open()` read it, and the match ignores 1–5, E, Space, R, Y, N and Esc while it holds (the overlay's own Esc closes it first; the selection underneath stays).
- **Esc in the match is read in `_input`**, before the GUI: Godot's viewport spends a `ui_cancel` press on hiding a visible tooltip and marks it handled (`Viewport::_gui_input_event`), so with the mouse resting on a card (cards have tooltips) the first Esc never reached `_unhandled_input`. The event is left unhandled, so the tooltip still closes in the same press.
- **Draw allocations**: `CardView` reuses one face `StyleBoxFlat` and `BoardView` builds its frame style once; every other style box drawn in `_draw` (key caps, ribbons, card backs, the CHECK tag, pips, score tracks, map caps) comes from `UiTheme.shared_box()` (the score track's animated flare and the shield badge's points are members reused per draw instead), a cache keyed by its arguments (never modified after creation).
- **Fixed match layout**: the side columns never grow with content (the opponent's hand row, `OpponentHand`, overlaps its cards only when they would not fit the column), and the escape prompt uses an eyebrow title and 4 px spacing so it fits the desk's height; a test checks the board's rect is unchanged when the prompt appears.
- **Busy hints**: the hint switches the moment an action starts ("Playing Knight…", "Redrawing", "Ending turn", the escape / stay answer) and shows the opponent's card while it plays; while the opponent answers the player's check the hint says so under the "Your turn" header.
- **Float texts in the match** (iteration 2): `MatchFx._float` places each text on the first spot around the action (above, diagonals above, sides, below, then one further out) whose swept area (text + rise) covers no piece glyph and no other live float, clamps it inside the grid, and rises only 0.35 of a square; a new turn removes any still on the board. It is MatchFx's own helper because `Feel.float_text` only clamps x and knows nothing of squares.
- **Toasts** are never wider than the board (they wrap) and sit on the half of the board away from the square where the action happens.
- **Discarded cards** leave the hand's layout when their sweep ends, so a held card re-centres.
- **Small cards with an upgrade ribbon** keep the same glyph circle as every other card (critique 3: the shrunken glyph broke the run end's deck row). The ribbon sits top right beside the cost coin whenever it fits (BOOK and HOLD on the run end's 72 px cards); only when it can't (CHEAPER on a 72 px card) it moves under the coin, left-aligned with it, over the circle's rim. `CardView.show_cost = false` hides the coin on mini cards too small for a readable figure (the roster's opponent deck); the cost is then in the tooltip, and the ribbon may use the top-left corner.
- **Deck / collection overlays** are keyboard-readable: every item takes focus (cards lift, piece rows get a brass ring) and its tooltip text is written in the line under the list, for focus and hover alike.
- **Taller windows (16:10)**: framed menu screens and the run end keep their 16:9 layout and take the extra height as equal top and bottom margin (`Ui.centre_tall`). The map already fills the height and is unchanged.
